Porsche 911 GT3 RS – Speed Meets Style

The Porsche 911 GT3 RS is a high-performance, track-focused version of the iconic 911 sports car. Known for its lightweight construction, aggressive aerodynamics, and naturally aspirated 4.0-liter flat-six engine, the GT3 RS delivers razor-sharp handling, thrilling speed, and extreme precision. Built with cutting-edge motorsport-derived technology, this machine is made for those who crave pure driving emotion. Its massive rear wing, lightweight carbon body, and race-tuned suspension make it a beast on the track while staying road-legal. From the roar of its engine to the confidence it gives in every corner, the GT3 RS is designed for serious enthusiasts who live for performance, control, and the ultimate driving thrill—both on the road and the racetrack.

Top Speed 296 km/h (184 mph)
